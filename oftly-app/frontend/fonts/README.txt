Bundled fonts (all licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; full texts alongside).
Source: https://github.com/google/fonts (ofl/fraunces, ofl/instrumentsans, ofl/atkinsonhyperlegible).
Files here are Latin subsets converted to WOFF2 (glyph coverage U+0020-024F plus common punctuation); other scripts fall back to system fonts.
Fonts are served from this app, so no request goes to a font CDN.
